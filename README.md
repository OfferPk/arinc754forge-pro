## 1\. Executive Summary

**ARINC754Forge Pro (v1.0.0)** is an enterprise-grade avionics Low-Range Radio Altimeter (RadAlt / LRA) workstation, FMCW radar signal processing simulator, RTCA DO-399 5G C-band RF interference analyzer, Autoland flare law engine, and automated compliance verification auditor implementing **ARINC Characteristic 754 / 754A** (Low Range Radio Altimeter), **RTCA DO-155** (MOPS for Low-Range Radio Altimeters), **RTCA DO-399** (Aircraft Radar Altimeter 5G C-Band Interference Susceptibility Testing and MOPS), **FAA AD 2023-12-12**, **FAA TSO-C87b**, and **ARINC Specification 429**.

The Radio Altimeter is the single most safety-critical low-altitude distance sensor aboard modern commercial transport aircraft (Boeing 737/777/787, Airbus A320/A330/A350/A380). Operating between 4.2 and 4.4 GHz in the Aeronautical Radionavigation Service band, the radar altimeter computes absolute terrain clearance from 0 to 2,500 ft AGL. It directly commands **Category-III Autoland Flare** (pitch-up guidance triggered at exactly 50 ft AGL), autothrottle **RETARD** command (idle thrust at 27 ft AGL), ground proximity warnings (TAWS/GPWS Mode 1 excessive sink rate), and cockpit voice callouts.

Between 2021 and 2024, the deployment of commercial 5G C-band cellular telecommunication towers (3.7–3.98 GHz) adjacent to major airports caused widespread avionics receiver front-end saturation, prompting FAA Airworthiness Directives (AD 2021-23-12 and AD 2023-12-12) mandating retrofits of 5G-tolerant bandpass filters across thousands of transport airframes. Historically, qualifying RadAlt software and testing 5G intermodulation interference required multimillion-dollar anechoic RF test chambers and specialized FMCW delay generators (\$90,000–\$280,000 per laboratory unit). **ARINC754Forge Pro** eliminates this barrier with a zero-dependency, dual-runtime (browser-native HTML5/ES6 and headless Python 3 CLI) workstation that models linear FMCW beat frequencies (fb \= 4h*DeltaF*fm / c), validates RTCA DO-399 40 dB 5G C-band filter rejection, calibrates residual antenna cable delay, tests autoland flare/retard state machines, and encodes bit-accurate ARINC 429 digital words (Labels 164, 165, 270, 377\) with Bit 32 odd parity.

## 3\. Signal Processing & Autoland Flare Physics

### 3.1 Linear FMCW Radar Beat Frequency Math

The radar altimeter transmits a linear frequency-modulated triangular chirp:

* Carrier Frequency: fc \= 4.3 GHz (4.2 to 4.4 GHz)  
* Sweep Bandwidth: DeltaF \= 100 MHz (10^8 Hz)  
* Sweep Repetition Rate: fm \= 100 Hz (Tm \= 10 ms)  
* Speed of Light: c \= 983,571,056 ft/s (299,792,458 m/s)

The beat frequency fb generated by mixing the received reflected chirp with the local oscillator is directly proportional to absolute height: fb \= (4 \* h\_raw \* DeltaF \* fm) / c h\_raw \= (fb \* c) / (4 \* DeltaF \* fm) (For example, at 100 ft height, fb ≈ 4,066.8 Hz).

### 3.2 Antenna Feedline Delay & Residual Height Calibration

With main gear compressed onto runway asphalt, the antennas remain 10–18 ft above ground plus coaxial cable delay (≈ 12 ns delay): h\_indicated \= h\_raw \- h\_residual Where h\_residual \= 16.0 ft, calibrating indicated altitude to exactly 0.0 ft at touchdown.

### 3.3 RTCA DO-399 5G C-Band (3.7–3.98 GHz) Rejection

* Unmitigated Threshold: Legacy altimeters suffer LNA intermodulation distortion when out-of-band 5G power \> \-45 dBm/MHz.  
* DO-399 Filtered Rejection: Requires \>= 40 dB attenuation across 3.7–3.98 GHz, maintaining linear dynamic range even within 1,000 ft of high-power airport 5G base stations.

### 3.4 Autoland Flight Control Flare & Retard Laws

* h \> 100 ft: Standard approach mode.  
* h \<= 50 ft: Autoland flare initiate (+2.5° pitch-up command on Flight Control Computer).  
* h \<= 27 ft: Autothrottle RETARD command initiated (throttles to idle).  
* h \<= 0 ft: Touchdown, rollout guidance, and wheel spin-up ground spoiler actuation.
